Discovery

The customer asked for a survey integration. The real problem was different.

The opportunity started when an existing WhatsApp4Dynamics customer enquired about integrating survey responses within Dynamics 365 CRM.

Through further discussion, I identified that the deeper problem was not simply survey integration. Sales agents were spending time asking prospects the same basic questions repeatedly before they could actually help them.

A typical WhatsApp interaction could start with a simple "Hi". The agent then had to manually collect information such as the prospect's name, product of interest and reason for contacting the organization.

This created unnecessary manual effort and delayed the point at which the sales or service conversation could actually begin.

Problem Reframing

From "integrate survey responses" to "automate information collection"

Rather than building only the requested integration, the product opportunity was reframed around automating the collection of prerequisite information before the sales agent responded.

Research

Validating the solution direction

I combined customer conversations with competitive research to understand how the feature should work and where existing solutions fell short.

Customer Interviews

Spoke with customers to understand the workflow, pain points and information agents needed before responding to prospects.

Direct Competitors

Reviewed competing Dynamics 365 solutions and identified questionnaire capabilities that were less user-friendly.

Indirect Research

Researched third-party applications to gather ideas for questionnaire configuration and interaction patterns.

Product Definition

Designing one configurable workflow for multiple use cases

The questionnaire flow needed to work across different organizations and business scenarios. Instead of hard-coding a fixed set of questions, I designed the configuration to allow WhatsApp4Dynamics customers to define their own questionnaire and question flow.

The configuration was designed to support use cases across sales, customer service and marketing.

Product definition

  • Created the PRD.
  • Created user stories and acceptance criteria.
  • Defined the questionnaire configuration.
  • Defined the user workflow.
  • Created Figma prototypes.

Configuration principle

  • Customers control the questions.
  • Customers define the questionnaire flow.
  • Supports different business scenarios.
  • Reduces repetitive agent interactions.
  • Creates a more structured WhatsApp conversation.
Product Strategy

The biggest product trade-off

Simplicity vs. Feature Completeness

Start simple, validate adoption, then expand

We considered how much configuration flexibility to include in the initial release. A more advanced configuration could support additional use cases, but would increase complexity and extend the release timeline.

Option considered

  • More advanced configuration options.
  • Greater flexibility from day one.
  • More feature completeness in the initial release.

Decision

  • Prioritize simple configuration.
  • Launch within the planned release timeline.
  • Validate adoption before expanding the feature.

Customer & Business Value

Less repetitive questioning. Faster, more structured conversations.

By collecting prerequisite information automatically, the feature reduced repetitive manual interactions for agents and created a smoother experience for end customers using WhatsApp. This supported faster response workflows, SLA adherence and more efficient customer conversations.

Reflection

What this experience taught me

Solve the root problem, not just the request.

A customer request for survey integration revealed a broader workflow problem around repetitive information collection.

Configuration can make an MVP scalable.

Making the questionnaire configurable allowed one feature to support different sales, service and marketing scenarios.

Adoption is a powerful validation signal.

Strong adoption among new and existing customers provided evidence that the simplified MVP solved a meaningful customer problem.
