01 · Executive Summary

Turning duplicated, fragmented CRM data into Data Privacy Laws compliant, AI ready foundation

A global data transformation initiative for a renowned global MedTech enterprise specializing in diabetes care. Designed for strict compliance with data privacy regulations (GDPR, HIPAA), the project aimed to improve patient data consistency, enhance operational efficiency, and strengthen the reliability of downstream AI applications.

The Global Healthcare Enterprise operated global CRM tenants containing ~10 million records across Europe, the USA, and APAC/LATAM. As the organization expanded from blood glucose monitoring to continuous glucose monitoring, fragmented patient, clinic, and pharmacy records created operational inefficiencies and weakened the quality of enterprise data.

As AI Strategist, I architected the rollout of DeDupeD across six environments using a hybrid architecture: deterministic algorithms for duplicate detection, Azure OpenAI for post-detection record resolution, and a native rollback engine for recovery and auditability.

The reported outcome was the cleansing of more than 1.2 million duplicate records, a reduction in duplication from 14.2% to below 0.8%, and an increase in Microsoft Copilot response accuracy from 62% to 94%.

03 · Strategic Challenge

Duplicate records were creating more than a data quality problem

Standard exact-match deduplication was insufficient for complex, multi-channel patient and business records.

01

Fragmented patient journeys

A single patient could exist across multiple records. Support agents lacked a unified interaction history, resulting in repeated troubleshooting and delayed CGM replacement workflows.

02

Unreliable predictive analytics

Split transaction histories affected the quality of patient churn prediction and sensor refill cadence analysis.

03

Inconsistent AI context

Duplicate and conflicting records affected Microsoft Copilot responses, creating inconsistent answers for sales and customer service workflows.

04

Unviable full-scale AI detection

Applying vector embeddings or semantic AI search across 9.5 million records in three international instances was considered financially and computationally unviable.

Baseline data duplication rate: 14.2%. The strategic challenge was to improve data quality at enterprise scale without introducing unnecessary AI compute costs or increasing the risk of healthcare data corruption.

1

Rule-based duplicate detection

The detection engine used exact matching for phone numbers, national or patient identifiers, and email addresses. Fuzzy name matching used Double Metaphone and Jaro-Winkler algorithms. The source brief reports that this approach identified approximately 95% of duplicates at minimal compute cost.

Exact matching Double Metaphone Jaro-Winkler Deterministic logic
2

AI-powered master selection and field-level merging

Once duplicate clusters were identified, the InoWiz Azure OpenAI Merge Engine evaluated conflicting attributes. It used temporal precedence logic to select appropriate master record values and generated confidence scores with plain-language reasoning.

Example reasoning: selecting an address because it matches the most recent pharmacy sensor shipment record.

Azure OpenAI Field-level reasoning Confidence scoring Temporal precedence
3

Native rollback and audit safeguards

Before merge execution, the rollback engine captured JSON snapshots of child entities, activity histories, and field values. This supported restoration if a merge was disputed and reduced the risk of irreversible record changes.

JSON snapshots Auditability Recovery workflow Non-destructive operations
05 · Strategic Decision Making

Why a hybrid AI strategy was the right architecture

The solution deliberately avoided applying generative AI to every stage of the workflow. It allocated AI to the decisions where contextual reasoning added value.

Strategic focus Alternative considered Selected approach Decision rationale
Detection methodology Full AI / semantic vector search 100% rule-based exact and fuzzy matching Controlled compute and token costs across 9.5M records.
Architecture boundaries External Python / AWS LLM pipeline Native Dataverse + Azure OpenAI Designed to keep processing within the stated Microsoft BAA boundary.
Automation vs. control Unrestricted background bulk merging Tiered confidence and human-in-the-loop Prioritized precision and review controls over raw automation speed.
System intake Real-time hard blocking Soft warnings and off-peak batch cleansing Balanced data quality with support workflow continuity.

Confidence-based decision framework

Strategic principle: AI should be applied where it provides differentiated reasoning value. Deterministic algorithms remain preferable for high-volume, well-defined matching tasks.

08 · Strategic Takeaway

Making enterprise healthcare data ready for responsible AI

The central strategic insight was that enterprise AI performance depends on the quality and consistency of the data foundation beneath it.

This case demonstrates a practical approach to healthcare AI transformation: use deterministic methods for scalable and explainable matching, apply generative AI selectively for complex resolution decisions, and embed recovery and human oversight into the architecture.

By treating data hygiene as an enabler of enterprise AI rather than simply a CRM maintenance task, the initiative established a stronger foundation for AI-assisted customer service, predictive analytics, and patient engagement.
