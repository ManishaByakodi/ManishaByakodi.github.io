01 · THE OPPORTUNITY

Finding an AI opportunity inside Dynamics 365

The product originated from an internal strategic objective: build an AI product that could demonstrate the organization's AI capability and expertise.

I was involved in identifying gaps within the existing Microsoft Dynamics 365 ecosystem. One opportunity stood out: recommendation and Next Best Action capabilities that could help sales representatives decide what to do next.

The opportunity was further validated when a customer enquired about an AI product that could recommend actions to sales representatives to improve conversion from leads to opportunities, as well as recommend products for cross-sell and upsell opportunities.

Opportunity reframed: How might we use AI to help Dynamics 365 users determine the most impactful next action for a customer or record?
02 · DISCOVERY

Understanding the seller's workflow

I spoke with sales representatives to understand their day-to-day challenges while working in CRM and documented recurring pain points.

Manual prioritization

Sales and account teams spend time manually deciding which leads and opportunities deserve attention.

Static workflows

Rule-based workflows do not easily adapt to changing customer behavior, sentiment or engagement.

Limited contextual guidance

Users often interpret dashboards and reports instead of receiving clear guidance directly in their workflow.

Trust in AI

Recommendations are difficult to operationalize when users cannot understand why an action was suggested.

Product opportunity: Turn Dynamics 365 from a passive system of record into an active, AI-guided system that helps sellers decide what to do next.
03 · MARKET & COMPETITIVE RESEARCH

Finding whitespace in an emerging AI market

I conducted gap analysis and competitor research across the Dynamics 365 ecosystem and adjacent CRM products.

The research included Salesforce Einstein, Zoho Zia, HSO's recommendation product and other Next Best Action products available through Microsoft Marketplace.

I also assessed Microsoft's emerging Copilot capabilities across CRM modules to understand where recommendation capabilities were heading.

04 · PRODUCT STRATEGY

Recommendation Engine vs. Next Best Action

One of the most important product decisions was deciding which AI capability should be prioritized for the initial product.

OPTION 01

Recommendation Engine

Recommend what a sales representative could offer or do — such as suggesting a product for a potential cross-sell or upsell opportunity.

OPTION 02 · SELECTED

Next Best Action Engine

Recommend what the user should do next, considering the broader CRM context and customer or record signals.

Strategic decision: We placed the Recommendation Engine on hold and prioritized Next Best Action. Competitive research indicated that recommendation capabilities were increasingly appearing across Microsoft's CRM modules, while Next Best Action represented a stronger opportunity to establish an early differentiated position.

11 · LAUNCH STRATEGY

Establishing an early AI footprint

The objective was not simply to launch another Dynamics 365 add-on. The broader strategic goal was to establish an AI product footprint and demonstrate the organization's capability in the emerging Dynamics 365 AI ecosystem.

Market timing was an important consideration. Microsoft was progressively introducing Copilot-driven recommendations across CRM modules, creating a need to establish a differentiated Next Best Action position early.

Strategic objective: Bring a differentiated Next Best Action capability to market while the Dynamics 365 AI landscape was rapidly evolving.

13 · LEARNINGS

What I learned building an AI product

AI strategy starts with a real workflow problem

The strongest opportunity emerged when AI capability was connected to a real seller workflow: deciding what to do next.

Competitive research can shape product strategy

The decision to prioritize Next Best Action over a Recommendation Engine was influenced by market direction and competitive timing.

Explainability is part of the product experience

AI recommendations need to provide enough context for users to understand why an action was suggested.

0→1 extends beyond product development

Building a new product required thinking across ideation, research, product definition, UX, UAT, release and market communication.

PM takeaway A successful 0→1 AI product requires more than selecting an AI capability. It requires finding the right problem, choosing where to differentiate, defining a focused product experience and building enough trust for users to act on AI-generated recommendations.
