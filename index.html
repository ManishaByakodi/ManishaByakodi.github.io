Perspective / 04

A PM who connects the dots.

Business analysis taught me to understand the detail. Product management gave me the opportunity to connect that detail to a bigger product vision.

My journey into product management started in business analysis, where I developed a strong foundation in understanding business problems, translating requirements and working with cross-functional teams.

I moved into product management to take greater ownership of the problem-to-product journey — from identifying opportunities and defining solutions to validating products and taking them to market.

Today, my experience sits at the intersection of AI, enterprise SaaS, CRM and product strategy. I’m particularly interested in products that make complex workflows more intuitive, useful and measurable.
