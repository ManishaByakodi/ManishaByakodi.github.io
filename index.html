Product Thinking

How I think about products

🔎 Start with the problem

I use customer conversations, research and data to understand the problem before jumping to a solution.

🎯 Prioritize for impact

I balance customer value, business impact, engineering effort and time-to-market.

🚀 Ship → Learn → Iterate

I prefer focused MVPs that validate the core problem before investing in unnecessary complexity.

🤖 Build responsibly with AI

I think about AI through user value, evaluation, reliability, guardrails and measurable outcomes.

📈 Measure adoption

A feature isn't successful because it shipped. I look at whether customers actually use it and derive meaningful value.

🤝 Work cross-functionally

I translate customer and business needs into clear product direction for engineering and stakeholders.
